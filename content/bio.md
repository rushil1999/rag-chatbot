# Who Rushil Shah is

Rushil Shah is a full-stack software engineer based in San Jose, California. He currently works
at Tesla as a Software Development Engineer, where he has been since February 2024. He has
roughly five years of professional software engineering experience across Tesla, Fetch Rewards,
Procure Networks, InfluxData, Shipmnts, and Taasha Technologies.

Rushil's core strength is backend and distributed systems work: low-latency services, event-driven
architectures, data pipelines, and making existing systems measurably faster and cheaper. A
recurring theme across his roles is taking something that already works and cutting its cost —
40% fewer Elasticsearch write errors at Tesla, CPU utilization from 95% to 32% at Fetch Rewards,
50% less external API consumption at Shipmnts, 20-25% lower search latency at Procure Networks.

He works most heavily in Go and Python today, and has shipped production code in TypeScript/Node,
Java, Elixir, and Ruby. He is comfortable across the stack — React on the frontend, Kafka and
RabbitMQ for messaging, MongoDB, DynamoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, Elasticsearch, and Snowflake for
storage, and AWS for infrastructure.

More recently Rushil has moved into AI engineering. He built and operates Vini, the
retrieval-augmented generation chatbot answering these questions, and Glide, a token-streaming
LLM chat backend. He is particularly interested in applying AI to sustainability and
positive-impact problems.

Rushil holds a Master of Science in Software Engineering from San Jose State University and a
Bachelor of Technology in Information and Communication Technology from Ahmedabad University in
India.
